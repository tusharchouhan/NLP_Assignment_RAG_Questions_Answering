ANH is an individual who has been assigned a 12-digit Aadhaar number by UIDAI.

Is this app available in other official languages?

Scenario 1: If the Aadhaar Number Holder is registered on the Aadhaar app using
the same mobile number that is registered with Aadhaar, ANH will be required to
verify the new mobile number with OTP, perform Face Authentication, and pay the
prescribed charges
Scenario 2: If the Aadhaar Number Holder is registered on the Aadhaar app using
a different mobile number from the one registered with Aadhaar, ANH will be
required to verify both the old registered mobile number and the new mobile
number with OTP, perform Face Authentication, and pay the prescribed charges
Scenario 3: If the Aadhaar Number Holder does not have access to the old
registered mobile number or has not registered a mobile number earlier, ANH
must visit the nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or an authorised Aadhaar enrolment
and update centre to update or register their mobile number.

Open Aadhaar App, login with your 6-digit PIN, then go to Services → Share Contact and

How to add the profile of a minor in the app?
A minor (below 18 years of age) can be added to the Aadhaar app if their relationship with the
guardian was established through biometric authentication at the time of Aadhaar
enrolment. The minor enters their Aadhaar number, after which the guardian completes face
authentication. Once the guardian’s face authentication is successful, the minor’s profile is
added to the app. If the Mandatory Biometric Update (MBU) of the minor has already been
completed, the minor will perform face authentication instead of the guardian.

Can this app be used outside India?
No, the Aadhaar app cannot be used outside India.

Why does the app need different permissions, such as camera, SMS, and call ?
Call and SMS permissions are required for SIM-based device binding, similar to payment
apps. These permissions help identify the active SIM and securely send or read verification
SMS. Camera permission is required for face authentication and verification to access
Aadhaar services.

How can the Aadhaar Verifiable Credential file be read?
Aadhaar Verifiable Credentials are machine-readable files that can be shared via WhatsApp or
email. They can be read using UIDAI-issued tools and prescribed technical standards.

How is consent management handled in the Aadhaar app?
Consent management in the Aadhaar app is governed by the Aadhaar Act and other
applicable laws. No identity data is shared without the explicit consent of the Aadhaar
Number Holder.

If data is updated by an Aadhaar Number Holder(ANH), how soon is it
reflected in the Aadhaar App?
The processing time for mobile number and/or address updates is up to 30 days. Once the
update is successfully completed, the revised data is reflected in the Aadhaar app.

Will secure QR codes on PVC Aadhaar cards and e-Aadhaar continue to work,
or will they be phased out?
QR codes on PVC Aadhaar cards and e-Aadhaar will continue to work and can be scanned
easily using the Aadhaar App or Aadhaar QR Code scanner for identity verification.

Technically, what standard does the Aadhaar QR follow, and can any app read
it?
The QR-based verifiable presentation follows a UIDAI specification and OPENID4VP, but the
reading capability is limited to the Aadhaar App, Aadhaar QR Code scanner only.
