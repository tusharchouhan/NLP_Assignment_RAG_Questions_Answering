Standard Operation Procedure (SOP) for carrying out name update in Aadhaar

1. The following may be referred to:

a) Regulation 19 (Update of information of Aadhar number holder) of the Aadhaar
(Enrolment and Update) Regulations, 2016,

b) Schedule II (List of acceptable documents for enrolment and update) of the
Aadhaar (Enrolment and Update) Regulations, 2016.

2. During the process of Aadhaar enrolment, demographic information of an individual
such as name, date of birth, gender, address is recorded based on the documents submitted, as
per Schedule II. Name in Aadhaar shall be recorded in block letters without the use of alias or
urf, prefixes, military ranks, designations, service, awards/decorations etc. Dots and other
special characters shall not be permitted in the name.

3. An Aadhaar Number Holder (ANH) may need to update/correct name as recorded in
Aadhaar due to various reasons. The ANH, seeking to update name, shall submit the request
accompanied by the corresponding documents as laid down under Regulation 19 ibid. The
name shall be updated as per the spelling, sequence, abbreviation etc. mentioned in the
submitted document.

4. An indicative list enumerating different scenarios applicable for name update, along
with prescribed acceptable documents is attached as Annexure A.

5. The newly requested name shall be matched with all the previously recorded/ updated
names of ANH. In case such matching results in complete change in name / change of
identity (categorized as a Major Name Change), the update request is liable to be rejected, if
not accompanied by a Gazette Notification.

6. The genuineness of submitted Proof of Identity (Pol) / Proof of Relationship (PoR)
documents, Affidavits and Gazette Notifications shall be verified while processing the update
request.
